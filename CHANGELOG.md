# Patch notes

## v2.0: The County Run (2026-11-08)

Lot 9 grew. The raccoons got ambitious. The alchemy is finally real.

### Alchemy, for real this time
- **20 recipes.** Drop one piece of junk on a *different* piece of junk and, if it's a recipe, they
  brew into something new. Boot + tire = **Monster Truck Shoe**. Car + fridge = **Ice Cream Truck**.
  Nobody asked what's in the Mystery Stew.
- **Tools** are one-use and dropped on their target:
  - **Electric Soup** zaps a 3×3 up a tier;
  - the **Junk Magnet** pulls every matching piece together;
  - the **Bag Bomb** pops a 3×3 for cash;
  - **Tire Fire** burns a whole row;
  - and six more.
- **Treasures** complete *any* raccoon's order and pay big.
- **Finding them:**
  - hold a piece over a recipe partner and it bubbles **"?"**;
  - recipes you know show what they make;
  - the Almanac has a **Recipe Book** of riddles that carries over between runs;
  - Grandma Mange drops a hint on your second night.

### Momentum
- **Cascades.** A fresh merge next to its twin merges again, and again. Park your pairs side by
  side.
- **The Stench Streak.** Merge, brew and deliver quickly and the pay multiplier climbs.
- **The Raccoon Rave.** Fill the streak and for 8 seconds everything pays ×3, the Dumpster is free,
  the music doubles up and the raccoons dance.
- **It's louder and wetter:**
  - hitstop on big merges;
  - results pop up above your paw;
  - splat stains that stay on the dirt all night;
  - bigger shakes (with a **Reduce shake** toggle);
  - coin sprays;
  - raccoons hug what you hand them;
  - and on night 1, something falls off the truck.

### The run
- **A run is 10 nights across the County Dump:** Lot 9, The Marina and Mount Trashmore, each with
  its own look and a **boss night**:
  - **The Inspection:** the Possum condemns a cell every hour.
  - **Gull God Raid:** feed it a gull or it steals your best junk.
  - **Spring Cleaning:** build the Trash King.
- **The county map.** Pick your road: every step forks into two nights, each with its own rule and a
  reward (**LOOT**, **SALE**, **JUICE** or **ELITE**).
- **New night rules:**
  - **Potluck:** raccoons want treasures;
  - **Rave Night:** the streak fills twice as fast.
- **Contraband.** 25 rule-bending perks, 5 slots. A free pick after every night you pass. Build
  something stupid:
  - Compost Heap, Raccoon Mafia, Chain Gang (diagonal cascades), Alchemist's Apron (brews make two),
    Possum Bribe…
- **The Swap Meet is a shop now:** Contraband for sale, Bin Juice and brewed tools, rerolls, and
  selling.
- **Three strikes.** Fail a night and you retry it. Fail three and you're **FIRED**.
- **Foremen.** Unlock Linda, Grandma Mange and Lil' Pete as starting picks, each with their own
  Contraband.
- **Shorter nights** (about 3½ minutes) and **bigger money** (every number ×10).

### Daily Dump
One seeded night a day, the same for everyone, one try. Copy your score card to brag.

### Screen
- **The game fills your window.** No black bars, nothing cropped, at any window shape. The lot just
  keeps going.
- **Fullscreen:** press **F**, or use the button on the title screen or in the pause menu.
- New backdrops for The Marina (boats, a lighthouse, a crane) and Mount Trashmore (a very large
  mountain of trash).

### Small fixes
- The Crusher's pay label is no longer cut off.
- "TONIGHT" no longer runs into the rule.
- Added a favicon.

### Save
v1 saves keep their Almanac and start a fresh run.
