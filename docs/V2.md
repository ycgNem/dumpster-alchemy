# Dumpster Alchemy v2 — "The County Run"

Why: `docs/CRITIC3.md` (5.5/10 for a regular player). The skin is distinctive, but the mechanics
underneath are generic:
- Everything a newcomer sees is on screen by 2:40.
- No decision matters; a greedy bot is crowned 20/20.
- There's no alchemy, every run is the same run, and nothing pulls anyone back.
- Most people quit at night 2, 7-15 minutes in.

v2 keeps the cast, the tone, the board and the merge, and adds:
- **discovery** (real alchemy);
- **momentum** (cascades, streaks, Fever);
- **choices and variety** (a roguelite run across a county map, with drafted Contraband);
- **a visible future** (the map, locked districts, bosses, unlocks, a daily);
- a screen that **fills the window**.

Targets for a regular player:
- the first sitting runs 30-45 minutes (one run);
- "one more run" is about 40 minutes;
- about 3 hours to see everything;
- a reason to come back tomorrow (the Daily Dump and the recipe book).

## The pillars

### 1. Alchemy: recipes from *different* junk (owner: alchemy agent)
- **Recipes.** Dropping A on B, where A ≠ B and the pair is a recipe, **brews**:
  - both pieces go;
  - the result lands in B's cell with a big reveal: a freeze, a bubbling puff, a "NEW RECIPE!" slam,
    and the result popping up above the paw.
  - A pair that isn't a recipe still swaps (unchanged).
- **The hover.** Holding a piece over a recipe partner shows a bubbling **"?"**. Over a pair you've
  already brewed, it shows the **result icon**. Brewed pairs are remembered across runs.
- **Recipe Book.** It's a new tab in the Almanac:
  - 20 slots, each "??? + ???" with a riddle (below);
  - once found, the slot shows both ingredients, the result and what it does;
  - discoveries persist across runs and saves.
- **Grandma Mange** teaches the first recipe on the first night 2 ("Try the can in the takeout,
  dear.").
- **Results come in two kinds:**
  - **Tools:** one use, dropped on their target. They don't merge or swap.
  - **Treasures:** they satisfy **any** raccoon. Drop one on any order card: that order is complete,
    and it pays the treasure's value, times the order's pay. They don't merge or go smelly.
- **Values.** Treasure values are multiples of `junkValue` (so they scale with the economy). They
  should beat selling the two ingredients by about 1.5-2.5x. Tools are priced by use.
- **The list.** Recipes are symmetric (A+B = B+A). Item ids are as in `src/data/items.ts`. The new
  ids are fixed: the art agent draws them.

### 2. Momentum: cascades, the Stench Streak, Fever (owner: feel agent)
- **Cascades.**
  - When a merge result sits orthogonally next to an identical piece that is idle (not held, not in
    flight), they merge again after a visible beat of about 0.15-0.2 s, into the newer piece's cell.
    This repeats.
  - `mods.cascadeDiagonal` adds diagonal neighbours.
  - Each link is a real merge: stats, `noteItem`, gull chance, events.
  - Fx: "COMBO x2 / x3…", a rising pitch, and a bigger shake each link.
  - Time-stepped in state (`updateCombo`), deterministic, so the sim sees it. Placement now matters:
    park pairs next to each other.
- **Stench Streak.**
  - The window is 1.6 s plus `mods.streakWindowBonus`.
  - Merges, brews and deliveries inside the window raise the streak; letting it lapse resets it.
  - `streakMul(s)` multiplies delivery pay: x1 → x1.25 → x1.5 → `mods.streakMaxMul` (default x2).
  - HUD: a fly-swarm meter with the multiplier.
- **Fever, the "RACCOON RAVE".**
  - Filling the streak meter starts 8 s of:
    - deliveries at x3;
    - Dumpster clicks that cost no charge;
    - the music going double-time;
    - raccoons dancing on their cards;
    - a gentle colour pulse.
  - Target: an average (1.8 s) player gets 1-2 Fevers a night, a fast one 3-4.
- **Feel pass:**
  - hitstop (render-only, never in the sim) on tier-6+ merges, cascades and brews;
  - the result pops up above the paw, then settles;
  - **splat decals** stain the dirt for the rest of the night (tier-4+ merges, bombs);
  - shake up to 6 px on big moments, with a **Reduce shake** toggle in pause;
  - a coin burst across the screen on a jackpot or treasure;
  - the raccoon hugs or sniffs what you hand over;
  - on the first night of a run, around 3:30 AM, "SOMETHING FELL OFF THE TRUCK!": a guaranteed Bald
    Tire.

### 3. The run: a county map, Contraband, strikes (owner: run agent)
- **A run is 10 nights in three acts across the County Dump:**
  1. **Lot 9:** the First Shift, two nights, then the boss, **The Inspection** (Possum shuts you down
     at 75; he also condemns a cell each hour: no junk can land there).
  2. **The Marina:** two nights, then the boss, **Gull God Raid** (gulls galore; the Gull God circles
     and steals your best piece each hour unless the lot has a gull order filled since). The run agent
     may simplify this boss.
  3. **Mount Trashmore:** two nights, then the boss, **Spring Cleaning** (build the King, as now).

  The act sets `GameState.scene` ('lot9' | 'marina' | 'trashmore'), which picks the backdrop.
- **The map scene**, between nights:
  - a county map with the three districts left to right, the road of nodes, and "you are here";
  - at each step after night 1 you **pick one of two forks**: two nights with different rules from
    the act's pool and different **reward tags**: `loot` (+1 Contraband pick), `shop` (Swap Meet 25%
    off), `juice` (a Bin Juice), `elite` (quota +40%, rare loot);
  - you can see the boss ahead.
- **Rule pool:** the old nights' rules, plus at least two new ones:
  - "**Potluck**": raccoons ask for treasures and recipes, pay x1.5. This replaces Takeout Tuesday.
  - "**Rave Night**": the streak fills twice as fast.
- **Contraband** (Balatro jokers):
  - rule-benders, with **5 slots** per run;
  - after each passed night, a **free pick of 1 of 3**;
  - the **Swap Meet** becomes the shop: 3 Contraband for sale (common/uncommon/rare), 2 consumables
    (Bin Juice, a random tool), a reroll (price rises), and selling a Contraband for half;
  - the old six upgrades become Contraband (below);
  - most effects compile into `Mods`, and a few hook events.
- **Strikes:**
  - 3 per run (Possum write-ups);
  - failing a night is a strike: retry the night and keep half the Shinies, as now;
  - the third strike means **FIRED**: the run is over, with a stats card, unlocks earned, and New Run;
  - crowning the King wins the run.
- **Persists across runs:**
  - the Almanac, the Recipe Book, best scores, settings;
  - unlocks: more Contraband enters the pool via milestones (first boss, 5 recipes, first King…);
  - **Foremen**: start-of-run picks with a starting Contraband or twist (3-4 of them, unlocked by
    milestones).
- **Shorter nights:**
  - **about 35 s per in-game hour**, about 3.5 minutes a night;
  - the truck ramps from 1 AM;
  - retune with the sim.
- **Money explodes:** every value, quota and price is **x10** ("+60", "+1,760").
- **Daily Dump** (from the title):
  - one seeded night a day, with a random rule and 2 random Contraband, one try;
  - scored in Shinies;
  - the end screen copies a Wordle-style card, e.g.
    `Dumpster Alchemy Daily #12 · 🦝 48,120 · 👑 Fridge · 🧪 3 recipes · 🤢 63`;
  - no server.
- **Endless** may be cut (the Daily and runs replace it).
- **Save:** versioned. A v1 save keeps its Almanac and starts a fresh run.

### 4. The screen fills the window (owner: screen agent)
- **Flexible canvas.**
  - The 640×360 **stage** is always fully visible and centred.
  - The buffer grows in one dimension to the window's aspect (wide windows get more width, 16:10 and
    4:3 more height) and is drawn at the fit scale (drop the whole-number snap).
  - No ink bars, nothing cropped.
- **Coordinates.**
  - Scenes draw in stage coordinates through a translate.
  - Full-screen fills (dims, fades, vignettes, slam bands, the crowning) cover the **whole buffer**
    through one helper.
  - Pointer coordinates stay stage-relative.
- **Wide backdrops.**
  - Each scene's backdrop (`lot9`, `marina`, `trashmore`) is a wide panorama whose middle 640×360 is
    the stage layout: sky 0-56, lane 56-104, dirt below. It's anchored so drawing it at the stage
    origin fills the margins.
  - The menus use it too.
  - The Marina and Mount Trashmore are new: water, a pier and boats; a huge trash mountain with a
    throne on top.
- **A fullscreen toggle** (the F key, plus a button on the title and the pause menu) using the
  Fullscreen API. Hide it where the API doesn't exist.
- **Small fixes:**
  - "CRUSHER pays 1/tier" is clipped at the right edge;
  - "TONIGHT" runs into the rule text with no gap;
  - add a pixel favicon (`favicon.ico` 404s).

## As built: alchemy

**Files.** `src/data/recipes.ts` (the table, `ALCHEMY` numbers, `recipeFor`/`recipeOf`/`isTool`/`isTreasure`),

**State.**
- `GameState.discoveredRecipes: Set<ItemId>`: result ids ever brewed, seeded by `NewNight.recipes`.
  The run carries it as `Run.recipes` (meta keeps it across runs).
- `NightStats`: `brews`, `newRecipes`, `toolsUsed`, `treasures`, `treasureShinies`.
- `Order.stewed?`, `Order.riddle?` (the recipe a raccoon is hinting at).

**Events.**
- `brew {a, b, to, kind, c, r, fc, fr, first, twin?}`: combo.ts already reads it for the streak.
- `toolUsed {id, c, r, slot?, cells, made?, moves?, shinies?, stink?}`: fx already splats Bombs and
  Fires off it. Audio has no cases for either yet.

**Rules.**
- A brew removes both pieces; the result lands on the target. `mods.brewTwins` adds a copy in the
  nearest empty cell.
- Tools work when dropped on a piece (or a card, for the Stew). On dirt they move, on a wrong target
  they snap back, and the Crusher bins them. `toolPlan()` is the single source for the hover outline,
  the bot, and `useTool`.
- Treasures: `wants()` is true for any waiting order. `deliver` fills the whole order for
  `value × payMul` (the scrap x2 is left out), plus the patience bonus. The streak still applies.
- The Junk Magnet merges through `mergeInto` (real merges). Electric Soup never makes the King: he
  must be merged, so the crowning plays.
- The riddle drip is 14% per arrival, from night 2. The recipe's ingredients must already be in the
  Almanac.
- Grandma's tip shows on night 2+, once, when a can and a takeout box are both on the lot.
- Numbers (bomb x3 Crusher pay or x1 value, fire x2/x2 +6 Stink, coffee 20 s, fork x3, blanket −25,
  stew x2) are in `ALCHEMY`.

**Treasure values** are points on the junk curve, so they follow `value.scale`. Each is about 1.9-2.4x
its ingredients: Chime 130, Burrito 650, Piggy Boot 1,200, Rotisserie 4,100, Truck Shoe 6,900,
Getaway 21,000, Go-Kart 23,000, Shopping Bed 47,000, Panic Room 110,000, Ice Cream Truck 320,000.

- Per night: 5-12 brews (1-4 new), 0-3 tools used, 3-6 treasures, and 1.8k-4.4k treasure Shinies
  (5-12% of the purse).

- The Chime dominates (cap + can are always around), then Stew, Soup, Fork and Magnet.
